# **App Name**: EVgoMap

## Core Features:

- Smart Route Planner: Suggest optimal charging stops based on vehicle range, current battery, and charging speed, powered by AI tool.
- Charging Station Locator: Locate nearby EV charging stations with a search and filtering system, viewable on a list or interactive map.
- Service Center Finder: Locate trusted EV service centers with user ratings and Google Maps links.
- Personalized Savings Dashboard: Track financial savings and CO₂ emissions avoided compared to gasoline car.
- Vehicle Service History Log: Keep a detailed digital logbook of all EV maintenance and repairs.
- Community Hub: Connect with other EV owners to start discussions, ask questions, and share tips.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated blue (#4285F4) evoking reliability and forward-thinking tech. It's energetic without being distracting, suitable for a utility app focused on sustainable solutions.
- Background color: Light desaturated blue (#E8F0FE), offering a clean, calming backdrop that won't distract from key information.
- Accent color: Analogous purple (#673AB7), creating visual interest for key interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern and accessible feel across all text elements.
- Use consistent, clean, and modern icons to represent different charging station types and vehicle services.
- Employ a grid-based layout with clear spacing to ensure information is easily digestible and the app feels organized.
- Subtle transitions and animations to acknowledge user interactions and guide them through the app seamlessly.
